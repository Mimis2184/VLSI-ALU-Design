# 3-bit ALU VLSI Design & Power Analysis

Design and simulation of a **3-bit Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU)** developed as part of the VLSI Systems Design laboratory coursework at the National Technical University of Athens (NTUA).

The ALU supports four operations:

- AND
- XOR
- Addition
- Subtraction

## Design Flow

**DSCH Schematic Design → Verilog HDL → MICROWIND → CMOS Layout → Simulation & Verification**

The design was developed hierarchically, starting from a **1-bit ALU** and combining three units to construct the complete **3-bit ALU**.

Functional simulations were performed to verify all supported operations.

## Power Analysis

CMOS dynamic power consumption was also investigated with respect to:

- Operating frequency
- Rise and fall times
- Capacitive load
- Supply voltage (VDD)
